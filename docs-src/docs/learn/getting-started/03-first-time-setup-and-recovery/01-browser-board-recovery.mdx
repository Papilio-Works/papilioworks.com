## Do it

1. Insert a FAT-formatted microSD card.
2. Hold the ESP32's **BOOT** button while you plug the board into your computer with a USB **data**
   cable. That puts the board straight into its programming mode.
3. Open [papilioworks.com/board-recovery](https://papilioworks.com/board-recovery/) in
   Chrome or Edge.
4. In **Step 1**, type your WiFi name and password (optional, 2.4 GHz only), click **Connect USB**,
   pick the first port in the list, and click **Install / Recover Board**.
5. Work through **Step 2** (A2600 core) and **Step 3** (Papilio Splash ROM).

## You're done when...

The Papilio Splash ROM is running on your TV, and the page shows your board's IP address.

## Check yourself

1. The browser lists two serial ports for your board. Which one do you pick?
   - a) The higher-numbered one
   - b) The first one in the list; if it can't connect, try the other
   - c) Either; it doesn't matter
2. Where do your WiFi network name and password go?
   - a) To a Papilio Works server, which forwards them to the board
   - b) Written to the board's flash over USB, during Step 1
   - c) Stored in your browser
3. What does "Load Papilio Splash ROM" need before it works?
   - a) Only a USB connection
   - b) A FAT-formatted SD card in the board, and the board's IP from WiFi
   - c) The Papilio Loader desktop app

## If something went wrong

See the troubleshooting table in the docs' Flash the Firmware page, and the Troubleshooting
Toolkit module. If the install says the board didn't respond, unplug the USB cable, hold **BOOT**
while plugging it back in, and click **Install / Recover Board** again. If you skipped WiFi in
Step 1, add it later under **Advanced tools** at the bottom of the page.
