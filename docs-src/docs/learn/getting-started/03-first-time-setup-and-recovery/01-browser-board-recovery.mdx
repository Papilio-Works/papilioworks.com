## Do it

1. Insert a FAT-formatted microSD card.
2. Connect the board to your computer with a USB **data** cable.
3. Open [papilioworks.com/board-recovery](https://papilioworks.com/board-recovery/) in
   Chrome or Edge, and work through Steps 1-4 on the page.

## You're done when...

The Papilio Splash ROM is running on your TV, and the page shows your board's IP address.

## Check yourself

1. The browser lists two serial ports for your board. Which one do you pick?
   - a) The higher-numbered one
   - b) The lower-numbered one
   - c) Either; it doesn't matter
2. Where do your WiFi network name and password go?
   - a) To a Papilio Works server, which forwards them to the board
   - b) Written to the board's flash over USB
   - c) Stored in your browser
3. What does "Load Papilio Splash ROM" need before it works?
   - a) Only a USB connection
   - b) A FAT-formatted SD card in the board, and the board's IP from the WiFi step
   - c) The Papilio Loader desktop app

## If something went wrong

See the troubleshooting table in the docs' Flash the Firmware page, and the Troubleshooting
Toolkit module. If the board won't respond at all, hold **BOOT** while plugging in USB to
start it in factory boot mode, then begin again at Step 1.
