## One board, many machines

The Papilio Retrocade is an FPGA board that can **become** a classic computer. Load one design,
called a **core**, and it's an Atari 2600. Load another, and the same board is a Commodore 64.
Nothing about the hardware changes; only the design running inside the FPGA does. We want to
bring more classic machines to the Retrocade over time.

And it's not just for playing. The same board can run **things you make**: your own graphics on
the TV, chiptune sound, a game of your own, or hardware nobody has thought of yet.

## Three boards, one job each

| Board | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Retrocade board** | Connects everything to the outside world: HDMI video, stereo audio, 32 MB of SDRAM for game cores, a PMOD connector for add-ons, and an RGB LED |
| **Tang Primer 20K** (Sipeed) | The heart of it: a Gowin FPGA with about 20,000 logic cells, a chip you can rewire into whatever hardware a core describes |
| **ESP32-S3 SuperMini** | The helper: WiFi, Bluetooth gamepads, USB keyboards and gamepads, loading your games, and programming the FPGA. Its USB-C port is the one you'll normally use |

Because the ESP32 is on WiFi, Papilio Loader can send games to the board and program it without
a cable every time you want to try something new.

## Learn FPGA and ESP32 development with AI

Here's what makes the Retrocade different: it's a board to **learn AI-assisted hardware and
software development** with. You bring the idea. An AI assistant helps you write the
Arduino-style code for the ESP32 and design the hardware inside the FPGA. You stay in charge:
you review what it makes, test it in simulation, and watch it work on the real board.

We're also teaching the AI to work with the board directly: changing the LED, writing to the
screen, checking why something isn't working, and seeing the result for itself.

## Where we're headed

| Status | What |
|---|---|
| **Works today** | Atari 2600 and Commodore 64 cores; Bluetooth and USB controllers; loading games and programming the board over WiFi with Papilio Loader |
| **Building now** | Papilio libraries for HDMI video, retro sound chips (SID, YM2149, POKEY), the RGB LED and ESP32-to-FPGA communication, brought up to date for the Retrocade; AI that controls and checks the board through MCP; the **Papilio Code** course on building your own projects with them; more classic machines |
| **Exploring** | A **Fantasy Engine** for original retro-style games; a synthesizer built from classic sound chips; boards that play together over WiFi |

Some of this works today, and some of it we're building right now. You can follow along as we
build it on the Papilio Works YouTube channel.
